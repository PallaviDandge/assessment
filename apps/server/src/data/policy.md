# Content Approval & Maintenance Policy (v1.2)

## P1 Review service levels
Every submission enters the queue as Pending Review. Safety-critical content (PPE, sensor thresholds, emergency procedures) must be reviewed within 4 working hours. Operational content such as onboarding folders and checklists must be reviewed within 1 working day. Training media (videos, images, maps) must be reviewed within 2 working days. If an item breaches its service level, escalate it to the Site Supervisor.

## P2 How to review an item
Open the item from the queue and use Snapshot & Control to preview it. Check three things: accuracy against the current site configuration, completeness (no missing pages, frames or checklist steps), and that the content contains no personal data such as faces, badge numbers or phone numbers. Videos must be watched in full; spot-checking is not allowed.

## P3 Approving and rejecting
Approve only when all three review checks pass. To reject, choose Reject and write a reason of at least one sentence that tells the submitter what to fix. Rejected items return to the submitter's drafts. You may not approve an item you submitted yourself; reassign it to another reviewer instead.

## P4 Camera maps and restricted zones
Spatial layouts and camera maps are classified Internal. Before approving, confirm that restricted zones are marked in red and that no camera covers a private area such as a washroom or a changing room. Maps that show security blind spots must be approved by the Security Lead, not by a regular reviewer.

## P5 Sensor specifications and maintenance
Sensor specification documents must list the calibration date and the alarm threshold for every sensor. Gas sensors are recalibrated every 90 days and smoke sensors every 180 days. If a specification lists a threshold that differs from the value in the HMI Panel, reject it and raise a maintenance ticket instead of approving.
